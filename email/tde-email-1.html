You’ve been the student in all of these dance classes…

The floor wasn’t swept. The fans weren’t on in a hot room.

The teacher was busy filming a reel rather than helping students.

There were lots of long demos and no practical breakdown of steps.

And the nervous new person by the door? Nobody said hello.

None of these teachers were bad dancers. Many were incredible.

But dancing and teaching are two different skills, and nobody teaches you the second one.

I’ve danced for 25 years, from competitive hip hop as a teen to swing, burlesque and contemporary in my 20s, then contact improvisation, which I’ve taught in Asia for 7 years.

I’ve held rooms of 70 people at festivals and retreats. Before that, I ran my own ecommerce business for a decade.

So I put the other half of the job into one course: how to lead a room with warm authority, plan a class that flows, and handle the business side, from registration to pricing, like a pro.

✓Energetics and self-care as a teacher
