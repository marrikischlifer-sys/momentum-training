Every photo in this email is from Bali Dance Festival, where I was invited to teach rooms of 40 dancers for two days. A room full of people used to feel like too much for me. Here's what helps.

2. Know your timing
Plan how long each part runs, and keep an eye on the clock. Starting and ending on time builds trust.

3. Set the room first
Sweep the floor. Fans on. Playlist tested. A ready room does half the holding for you.

4. One clear signal
A mic, a clap, a bell. Choose one way to call the group back, and use it every time.

5. Let the structure lead
You don't have to perform confidence. Follow the plan, stay present, and let the room come to you.
