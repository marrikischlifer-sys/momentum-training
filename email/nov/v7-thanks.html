As the year winds down, I wanted to say thank you. Whether you came to a workshop, listened to the podcast or just read these emails, you're part of why I keep doing this.

This year I taught the 45th Momentum Contact Fundamentals workshop, directed and organized new contact dance films, and kept teaching in the Koh Phangan community, hosting regular jams and classes at Sati Yoga and Arcana Wellness.

Graduates of the facilitator training now teach in 6 countries, and two of my students met through contact, teach together and got married. That one still makes me smile.

Four ways to learn with us

Teaching Dance Essentials: leadership and the business of dance, for teachers of any style. Online.
Level 1, Momentum Foundations: self-paced, with a 1:1 coaching call. Online.
Level 2, Momentum Facilitator Training: February 15–22, 2027 in Ubud, Bali.
Contact Body: online courses for dancers new to contact.

New to contact?

Keep posted for Momentum Contact Fundamentals workshops in January and February: Koh Phangan, Thailand in early January, and Ubud, Bali in mid-February. Upcoming events go up on Instagram and in my WhatsApp announcements group.

Visit momentum-contact.com

Wishing you a soft, rested end of the year. I look forward to dancing with you in 2027.

Dancingly,
Aalia
