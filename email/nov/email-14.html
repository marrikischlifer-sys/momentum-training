As the year winds down, I wanted to say thank you. Whether you came to a workshop, listened to the podcast or just read these emails, you're part of why I keep doing this.

This year I taught the 46th Momentum Contact Fundamentals workshop, made a new contact film, God in Details, on Leela Beach, and kept growing the facilitator training. Graduates now teach in 6 countries.

If you're looking for something to give someone who's curious about contact, Contact Body is my online school for dancers new to contact: two mini courses to learn the foundations at their own pace.

Explore Contact Body

And if 2027 is your year to start teaching, Level 1 is self-paced and ready when you are. Start Level 1.

Wishing you a soft, rested end of the year, and lots of good dances.
