I didn't start contact for the science. I started because it made me feel alive and less alone. Here are five things I think we're all missing, and why this dance offers all of them.

1. Play. Playful adults report lower stress, and cope with it better. Your inner child still wants to roll on the floor.

2. Dance. Dance is one of the most effective kinds of exercise for lifting our mood, and it builds balance, coordination and a healthy heart.

3. Authenticity. In a world of filters, contact asks one thing: be here, as you are.

4. Touch. Outside a sexual partner or a massage, most of us are touch deprived. Contact makes touch natural again: safe, consensual, playful, and without an agenda.

5. Connection. We're glued to our phones, and loneliness is real, especially if you're nomadic, travelling or far from family. We need ways to remember that people are just people.

So we put our phones down, take our shoes off, and dance. Want to try it at home?
