Two years ago you barely saw contact reels or tutorials online. Now more people find this dance every day, and I love it.

Here's how it's grown for me.

2020. My first workshop, in Ubud during the pandemic. I wrote it in 40 hours, 10 friends came, and no one knew it was my first time teaching. Half of them became long-term friends.

2021. I launched Contact Body, so people in lockdown anywhere could learn the foundations of contact.

2023. I started the Momentum Contact Facilitator Training. Graduates now teach in 6 countries.

Online can't replace a jam. But it can give you the skills, the structure and the confidence to start one.
