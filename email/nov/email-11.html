A room full of people used to feel like too much for me. Now I hold space for rooms of 70 dancers. I didn't become an extrovert. I built structure. Here's what helps.

1. Write it all down. My first workshop took 40 hours to write, and I've revised my Momentum manual twice a year for 7 years. When the plan is on paper, my nerves have somewhere to rest.

2. Know your timing. Plan how long each part runs, and keep an eye on the clock. Starting and ending on time builds trust.

3. Set the room first. Sweep the floor. Fans on. Playlist tested. A ready room does half the holding for you.

4. One clear signal. A mic, a clap, a bell. Choose one way to call the group back, and use it every time.

5. Let the structure lead. You don't have to perform confidence. Follow the plan, stay present, and let the room come to you.

Leading is a skill. It can be learned.
