God in Details

A sneak peek at a new art music video with the contact dancers from Koh Phangan.

We gathered on Leela Beach. In Sanskrit, Leela means “play,” the divine play of creation unfolding freely. We climbed trees, lifted and improvised, and this little film is what came out of it.
