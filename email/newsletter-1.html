I'm happy to share that applications are open for the 2027 edition of the Momentum Contact Facilitator Training.

Since 2020, Momentum has supported dancers, facilitators and movement teachers in learning how to structure and lead Contact Improvisation classes, workshops and jams. Graduates have joined from China, the United States, Russia, Portugal, Korea and Ireland.

It's a 50-hour hybrid training: you begin online, then train in person.
