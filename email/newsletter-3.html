There's an issue I see in almost every new contact teacher, and I had it too.

When we start teaching, we think we need to be the best dancer in the room. So we show our most impressive lifts, we try to have every answer, and we teach choreography instead of skills. The class looks good, but students leave without anything their body can use, and they don't come back.

After taking hundreds of dancers through their first steps in contact, here's what I've learned they actually need.

“

Dancing contact and teaching contact are two different skills. Momentum trains the second one.
