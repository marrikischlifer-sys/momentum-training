Many of you know me through contact improvisation. But I know many of you don’t dance contact at all.

You teach salsa, swing or pole. You DJ ecstatic dance. You’re a somatic educator, a movement coach, or you run a studio or a retreat. Or you’ve been dancing for years, and you’re finally ready to start your own classes.

This one is for you.

✓New dance teachers. You want to start your own classes, even if you’ve never taught.
✓Somatic educators. You want to hold a room, not just a session.
✓Ecstatic dance DJs and event organizers. You want big, open-level rooms that feel safe.
✓Movement coaches. You’re ready to go from online sessions to real rooms, in person.
✓Dance and yoga studios. You want stronger teachers and a smoother business.
✓Retreat leaders and program directors. You want groups that come back, year after year.

Teaching Dance Essentials is the leadership, facilitation and business part of my facilitator training, without the contact material. It’s what I wish someone had handed me before I taught my first class.

12 short lessons, about 4 hours in total, at your own pace. You can start the day you join.
