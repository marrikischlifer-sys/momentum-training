I’ve been quiet here since April. Since August I’ve been back on Koh Phangan, teaching in the contact community here, and I want to share again how Momentum began and where I am today.

In 2020, I got stuck in Ubud during lockdown.

I'd spent almost two years going from contact intensive to festival to intensive. I'd trained with Edam Dance Company and with Contact Beyond Contact. I had so much in my body, and no idea where to begin teaching it.

So I sat down and wrote out my entire first workshop. It took me 40 hours.

Ten of my friends showed up. I felt proud, and for the first time, a real sense of purpose.

Afterwards people kept telling me I must have been teaching for years. They had no idea it was my first workshop.

That preparation made all the difference. Since then I've run the Momentum workshop more than 45 times, adjusting and refining the work according to what my students actually need and grow from. I've held workshops and jams in 4 communities over 7 years, and taught at Georgia Silent Contact Festival, Bali Dance Festival, the Towards CI50 Conference, and private retreats.

And I keep seeing new teachers struggle with the same things nobody prepares them for:

✓A class that drags, or runs out of time
✓Being able to do the movement, but not to say it out loud while demonstrating
✓Not knowing how to invite someone who's nervous about dancing with others
✓No idea how to promote themselves online
✓Music that doesn't suit contact improvisation
✓A space too noisy to learn in, a floor nobody swept, no fans on in a hot room

The Momentum Contact Facilitator Training is everything I wish I'd had in 2020. Momentum started in Ubud, and I’m so happy to bring it back there in February.
