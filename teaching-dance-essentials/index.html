The skills are transferable

Whatever style you teach, your students need the same things from you: clear structure, a room safe enough to open up, and your presence.

Who it’s for

  • New dance teachers. You’ve been learning a style for years and now want to share it in your community and start your own classes. It’s perfectly fine if you haven’t started teaching yet.
  • Somatic educators guiding people back into their bodies
  • Embodiment and movement coaches moving from online sessions into teaching in-person classes
  • Ecstatic dance DJs, facilitators and event organizers who want to hold big, open-level rooms
  • Dance and yoga studios that need leadership and business skills for their teachers
  • Retreat owners and program directors leading groups, staff or a training program
