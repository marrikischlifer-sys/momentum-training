Before you teach

What your contact students actually need from you

They don't need

  • The most acrobatic dancer in the room
  • Your most impressive lift on a reel
  • Choreography to copy
  • A teacher with every answer

They do need

  • Contact skills broken down so their body understands
  • A room safe enough to open up to connection and conversation
  • Clear structure and clear consent
  • Your presence, permission and authenticity

Is this for you?

Who this training is for

  • A contact dancer who feels ready to start teaching or hosting jams
  • Wanting to grow a contact community where you live, even where none exists yet
  • A bodyworker, coach or therapist bringing movement into your work
  • Someone who wants structure, mentorship and real skills
  • Curious, willing to practice and ready to lead

Not for you yet, if you're

  • Only interested in performing, choreography or learning contact for yourself. A festival or workshop is the better fit.
  • Not ready to take responsibility for a group
  • Not ready to put in the practice, the reflection and the time

Meet Aalia Hashem

Contact Improvisation has guided my life since 2018. In 2020 I founded the Momentum workshop, and I've since taken hundreds of dancers through their first steps in contact, in Bali, Thailand, Canada and beyond.

Students describe me as accessible, authentic, technical and strong, while being fun, approachable and present.

My journey began at Edam Dance Company, learning from the pioneers of Canadian contact. I've studied with over 50 contact teachers, and my movement background spans 20 years, from contemporary and swing to bodywork and qigong. I'm a certified 200-hour Kundalini Hatha yoga teacher, and Contact Beyond Contact Level 1 first brought me to Ubud in 2019.

I host the Faces of CI podcast, founded the Contact Body online school, and direct dance films shown at Canada's Contact Dance Film Festival. I've been invited to teach at Bali Dance Festival, Georgia Silent Contact Festival, Towards CI50 Conference, Bali Bloom and Arcana Koh Phangan.

I've trained in facilitation and leadership with Rick Smith, an American teacher who has trained teachers across the United States and many of Bali's facilitators. Teaching runs in my family: my mom was a school and university teacher, and my dad taught driving, flying and hang gliding. In Human Design, my purpose is the Left Angle Cross of Education, a life devoted to teaching.

Before you apply

FAQs

Who can join Level 1?

Anyone who intends to teach contact or host jams. There's no experience requirement for Level 1. If you teach another dance style, Teaching Dance Essentials may be a better fit.

Why do I need a year of experience for Level 2?

Level 2 is by application, and we accept dancers who already have a contact practice of at least one year, about 40 hours, so they're invested in the form. In Dancing Deeper Still, Martin Keogh suggests around a year of dancing before teaching, and Aalia studied for nearly two years before she started.

Training to teach something you haven't embodied yet is like going to a yoga teacher training as a brand-new yogi. We want facilitators who carry what they share in their own bodies. Accepted dancers receive an acceptance email after the intake form.

Can I skip Level 1 if I already teach?

No. Even experienced contact teachers find new tools in Level 1, and it means everyone arrives in Bali with the same foundation in hosting, leadership and the business of dance.

Do you offer discounts or scholarships?

No. The early bird price for Level 2 is the only discount, and we don't offer scholarships, work-trade or exchange places.

Where is Level 2?

At our private studio at Rasa Yoga in central Ubud, Bali. Modern rooms are available on site for around 500,000 IDR a night, and booking them is optional. Rasa has a small café with coffee and matcha and an open kitchen for guests, but meals aren't provided.

The week is full, so there isn't much time for sightseeing. Aalia lived in Bali for four years and taught in Ubud and Uluwatu from 2020 to 2024, and she's happy to share local tips once you're accepted.

Are accommodation and food included?

No. Level 2 covers the training only, so you can stay on site or anywhere else in Ubud that suits you, from rice-field villas to simple homestays.

What does a week in Level 2 look like?

Momentum technique classes, facilitation labs and teaching practice, ending with a celebration together, like a sauna and dinner night out. You'll get the full schedule a week before.

What if I miss a live call?

Every call is recorded, so you can catch up in your own time.

Can I teach the Momentum Method afterwards?

Yes. Use any of the practices freely in your own classes. If you build workshops on this material, please credit it as the Momentum Contact Method and share that you trained with Aalia.

What is the refund policy?

All sales are final and non-refundable, so please book mindfully. If we cancel a training, you'll receive a full refund.
